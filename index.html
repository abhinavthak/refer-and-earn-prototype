Questions

Questions about your invite

What your friend's link gets you, in plain language.

How does the 10% discount work?

You opened a friend's invite link, so 10% off is already applied to the total fee of every programme. The price you see on this page is the price you pay.

Do I need a code?

No. The discount comes from the link. Just book your session from this page.

Does booking a session cost anything?

No. The counselling session is free and you are not committing to enrol.

What happens after I submit the form?

A counsellor calls you within 24 hours on the number you gave. They answer your questions and help you apply.

Can I change my programme later?

Yes. Your discount applies to whichever programme you finally enrol in.

Are these degrees recognised?

Yes. They are UGC-entitled online degrees with live classes, recorded lectures and placement support.

I've already spoken to the university. Can I still use this link?

The discount is for new applicants. If you are already an active applicant or student, your counsellor will tell you what applies to you.

Does my friend get anything?

Yes. When you enrol, your friend gets a gift card as a thank-you. It costs you nothing.

Rules enforced

  • Referrals count only when a friend signs up through the referrer's link. No codes, no manual adds.
  • Referrer cannot refer their own mobile number.
  • One referrer per friend. Duplicates and existing active leads are rejected.
  • A gift card is reserved when the friend pays the fee. It unlocks after the lock period. A refund inside that period cancels the gift card and later ones move up.
  • Tier position is decided by enrolment date, not referral date.
  • Payouts are automatic. A gift card is sent to the referrer's email and mobile the day its lock period ends, with no claim step.
  • Ops can change the amount of a scheduled gift card before it sends. Sent amounts are frozen.
